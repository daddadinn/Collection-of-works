About · 关于

daddadinn®

在画纸与像素之间，捕捉一瞬间的情绪 —— Less, but better.

daddadinn 是一名视觉创作者，作品横跨绘画、插画、海报与拼贴。相信克制的力量：一支颜料、一支铅笔、一块留白，就能讲完整一个故事。

A visual creator working across painting, illustration, poster design and collage — believing in the power of restraint: one pigment, one pencil, one piece of white space can tell a whole story.
